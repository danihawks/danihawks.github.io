«The individual builds himself a dwelling for the same reason that he has clothed himself... to protect himself from the weather and the dangers which surround him...» A. Lefèvre (1880) p.11

But what if the «dangers» were the city itself?

The entire structure is conceived as a permeable shell in which to find refuge. The city is clearly visible through the windows, beyond the verdant screen covering the main façade, yet it remains muffled, mitigated, and distant. The steel portal frame structure—which envelopes and supports the floor slabs—is designed to function as an additional “structural filter”.
