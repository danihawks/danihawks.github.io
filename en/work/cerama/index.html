Cerama

Politecnico di Torino

The masterplan stipulates that each building features an urban podium, designed to separate ground-floor retail units from the primary entrances of the various residential programs (market-rate, social, senior, and student housing). The podium functions simultaneously as a spatial threshold and as a catalyst for social interaction among the residents.

The “social multiplier” concept reaches its pinnacle within the communal spaces generated by inverting the stairwell. The building is defined by a dual-façade logic: the primary exterior, clad in ceramic to reference the neighborhood’s historical architecture, and the inner façade—more intimate and welcoming—where the materiality shifts to timber.
