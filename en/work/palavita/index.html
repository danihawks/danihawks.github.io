The workshop aimed to align with MVRDV’s methodology regarding Artificial Intelligence. We investigated the conscientious and effective integration of AI tools within architectural practice, moving beyond mere automation. This experience has fostered a more nuanced and mature perspective on the role of AI in shaping the future of architecture.
