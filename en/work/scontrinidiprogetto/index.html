This project explores how an architectural work can be decomposed and rebuilt through a critical manipulation of its references, rules and operative tools. Developed in 2025 at the Politecnico di Torino under Giovanni Corbellini, it is structured around two exercises and concludes with an editorial volume that gathers the process.

Project A — Order in Chaos

The first exercise investigated treaties and theoretical texts by relevant architects as a way to decode compositional tools. The resulting meta-project proposes a public library in Braccini Park: instead of a single block, the programme is fragmented into permanent technical clusters and fluid glazed fields. The enclosure remains in tension with the park, reconnecting the former surface parking area to the surrounding metropolitan landscape.

Project B — A Building Carved by Trees

The second exercise worked on the synthesis of an existing architecture. Rather than treating ecological protection as a constraint, the project uses the tree as a generative algorithm: trunk and crown become a spatial matrix that produces programmed erosions and a field of operational absences.

Image 1 / 4

The final configuration places the enclosure in a dynamic relationship with the existing vegetation. The building is shaped by the forces between the natural environment and the dense city, producing a singular architecture tied to the local flora.

Image 1 / 2

The Book

The process concludes with a cardboard volume and an editorial experiment. The book collects the two exercises as a graphic and spatial sequence, turning the project into a final object as well as a record of its making.
